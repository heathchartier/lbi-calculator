❓ How to Use LBI IQ

Getting Started

Every quote starts on the Job Information card at the top: fill in the Job Name / Project, Customer, PO / Quote #, Date, and any Notes. None of this affects pricing — it's just what prints on the quote and what you'll search for later in Saved Jobs.

Below that are five tabs — Veneer Panels, Solid Lumber, Stock Items, Lamination, Calculators — and a running Quote Summary at the bottom of the page that totals everything you've added across all four material tabs (Calculators never adds to the quote — see below).

Use the 🔒 Change Password icon (top right) any time to set your own password on this device. Each device remembers its own password — setting one here doesn't affect any other phone or computer, and nobody else can set a password for your device either.

Saving, Loading & Starting Over

💾 Save Job stores the current quote. Once it's saved, the button becomes 💾 Update Job and a green "✎ Editing saved job" note appears — any further changes update that same saved job instead of creating a new one.

Saved Jobs (top-right icon) lists everything you've saved — tap Load to bring one back up.

Once a saved job is loaded, the "+ New Job" button becomes ✕ Clear & Start New — use it to wipe the screen and start a fresh, blank estimate without closing and reopening the app. If you have unsaved changes it'll ask you to confirm first.

Veneer Panels Tab

Ceiling Type picks the calculation mode:

  • Ceiling Grille — the classic slat-and-panel layout. Set Orientation (Horizontal/Vertical), Finish, Grade, Panel Core, Thickness, and Species (only species with a price on file show up). Enter Panel Width/Length and Slat Width/Length, how many Slats per Panel, and Brackets per Panel.
  • Ceiling Tile — no orientation or slats. Enter the tile's real Tile Width/Length (this drives sheet cutting and material cost) and a separate Nominal Sq Ft / Tile — this is only used to size the Dado/Groove service (e.g. a 23.75"×23.75" tile is billed nominally as 4 sq ft for dado work, from a 24"×24" grid). The "Assembly" toggle is relabeled Dado / Groove for tiles.

Calculate By lets you enter a total Ceiling Sq Ft and let the tool work out panel/tile count, or enter an exact slat/tile/panel count yourself. Edge Band Sides controls how many edges get edge banding (4 sides down to none). Every config has its own +10% / +15% waste toggle (mutually exclusive — pick one or neither) added on top of the raw material need.

Click + Add Panel Configuration to add more panel types to the same job — sheet material is automatically pooled/nested across every config of the same species/grade/core/thickness to minimize sheets needed.

Solid Lumber Tab

Ceiling Type here has three modes:

  • Ceiling Grille — same slat/panel/bracket layout as Veneer Grille, but priced by the board foot. Enter Finished Width and Length, Slats/Panel, Panel Width/Length, Brackets/Panel.
  • T&G Ceiling — no panels/slats/brackets. Enter Face Width (the visible coverage width), Overall Width (the actual board width, wider than the face because of the tongue & groove), and Finished Length. Leave Finished Length blank in Sq Ft mode and the tool assumes random lengths.
  • Trim — same as T&G but for solid trim boards where the face and overall width are the same piece — there's no separate Face Width field, just Overall Width.

Every dimension field on this tab accepts decimals or fractions — type 3.25 or 3-1/4, whichever's easier. Toggles: Sanding, Cut to Length, and the waste pair, plus Assembly (Grille only — T&G and Trim never show it, since they're not assembled panels).

For V.G. Hemlock/Fir/Therm VG Hemlock, the tool automatically picks 2×6 or 2×8 rough stock based on the piece's width — you'll see a "Milled from 2×X" badge next to Finished Thickness reflecting that pick.

Stock Items Tab

A catalog of standard products (sheet goods, hardware, etc.) organized into collapsible categories — tap a category to expand it, or use the search box to jump straight to an item (search auto-expands matches). Enter a quantity next to any item to add it to the quote; the unit shown (EA or BF) tells you whether you're entering each pieces or board feet.

Lamination Tab

Pick a Laminated Face and (optionally) a different Laminated Back, a Core and Core Thickness, then Calculate By and dimensions just like the Veneer tab. The tool automatically finds the cheapest combination of sheet sizes across face, back, and core — including handling cores that only come in smaller "net size" sheets (like Baltic Birch) without you having to work that out by hand.

Calculators Tab

Quick standalone math tools — none of these add anything to your quote, they're just for checking numbers on the fly:

  • Board Feet — width × thickness × length × pieces, with a "Total (with waste)" line.
  • Sqft ↔ Linear Feet and Panel Count ↔ Sqft — bidirectional converters; fill in either side and the other fills itself in.
  • Slat Calculator — mirrors the Lumber tab's material/yield math (stock length, rough stock, raw BF) without needing a full panel config.
  • Tile Calculator — same idea for Ceiling Tile: sheet yield from net tile size, separate nominal sq ft reference.
  • T&G Calculator — mirrors the Lumber tab's T&G mode, including a V.G. Fir/Hemlock toggle since there's no species dropdown here.
  • Bracket Calculator — how many Baltic Birch sheets a given bracket size and count needs.

Reading Your Quote & Printing

The Quote Summary at the bottom breaks every config down into its own line items (material, edge banding, cut service, assembly/dado, brackets, etc.), then totals everything into one TOTAL ESTIMATE. Use the Print icon (top right) to get a clean, printable version of the quote.

⚙ Admin Guide

Everything above still applies to you — this section covers what only the Admin Pricing panel (gear icon) controls. Nothing here is visible when logged in with the LBI (customer) password.

Opening Admin Settings

Tap the gear icon (top right, only visible when logged in as admin) to open Admin Pricing. Everything you change there is saved to pricing.json and, if Cloud Sync is connected, pushed out to every device automatically the next time it loads.

Cloud Sync

Pricing data (species prices, margins, etc.) syncs automatically to every device — no setup needed, every phone/tablet running the app just picks up the latest pricing on next load. The Cloudflare Worker URL, Worker Key, and both passwords are different: for security, none of those distribute automatically anymore — each is entered once, per admin device, right here in Admin Settings, and stays local to that device only (same treatment the GitHub Token always got). If job saving/loading isn't working on a device, re-enter the Worker URL/Key here on that device. The GitHub Token is what actually lets you push pricing changes out (Save in Admin writes to GitHub, which every device then pulls pricing data — not credentials — from).

Passwords

Set your own Admin password and the shared LBI (customer) password here. Leave a field blank to keep it unchanged — you don't have to re-enter both every time you save.

Markup / Margin

Every category (Panels, Edge Band, Lumber, Milling, Assembly, EB Service, Cut Service, Brackets, and Dado/ Groove) has its own margin % field. These are true gross margin percentages, not markup — entering 15 means the sell price is cost ÷ (1 − 0.15), i.e. cost is 85% of the sell price, not cost + 15%.

Veneer Panel Pricing

The species table is organized by Finish (Standard/Satin) → Thickness → Core tabs. Each species has 8 price fields: Talbert/Timber (grade) × A3/AA (orientation) × 4×8/4×10 (sheet size). Leave a cell at $0 to hide that combination from the customer view entirely — species only show up on the Veneer tab if at least one price is set for the current Finish/Thickness/Core selection. Edge Banding Roll Costs are set per species further down the same panel.

Lumber Pricing

Standard species price by rough-stock tier: 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 8/4 ($/BF each) — the tool auto-detects which tier a job needs from the finished thickness. V.G. Hemlock/Fir species check the Resaw box instead and use the separate 2×6 / 2×8 price columns, since those always mill from 2" rough stock regardless of finished thickness.

Service Rates

Grouped by service: Standard Milling, Resaw Milling, Sanding, and Cut to Length each have a flat fee under a threshold (measured in linear feet) and a per-LF rate above it. Labor/Assembly holds the shared Assembly $/sqft and Bracket $/ea rates. Veneer Services holds EB Service $/ft, Cut Service $/sqft (with its own flat-charge/threshold pair, measured in sheets), and the Dado/Groove pair for Ceiling Tile (same flat/threshold pattern, measured in tiles instead of sheets). Lamination Services holds the Glue Line $/sqft rate.

Lamination Admin

Faces price across 4×8, 4×10, and 5×12; Cores price across 4×8, 4×10, 5×10, and 5×12 at every thickness. Check Net Size for any core that comes in true 48×96/60×120 sheets rather than oversized sheet goods (Baltic Birch, for example) — the tool trims 1/4" per edge for those automatically before cutting.

Stock Items / Product Management

Add, edit, delete, and reorder (drag & drop) products and categories from the admin product manager. Each product has a cost and its own margin %, independent of the category-wide margins above.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

  • Stock length rounds up to the nearest 8'/10'/12'/14' tier, but treats a length exactly a half inch under a tier (e.g. 119.5") as fitting that tier — that's the standard trim-allowance convention. A length that exactly matches a tier (120" flush) bumps to the next size up, since zero margin isn't enough to safely cut from.
  • Pricing syncs to GitHub — when pulling changes on this device, always check for a real pricing update from another device before overwriting anything.
  • The GitHub repo backing this app is public. Don't paste anything sensitive beyond what's already here into pricing fields or job notes.
